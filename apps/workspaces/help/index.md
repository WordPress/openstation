# Workspaces

A workspace is a desk of its own: which apps show on the dock, which windows it opens with and where, how windows open, and whether settings are hidden.

## Saving and using a workspace

Arrange the main desk, then **Save as new workspace**. Name the new workspace in its card. The saved-window titles help you choose a desk; **Go to desk** opens it and **Current desk** marks the one you are using.

**Customize** contains the icon, colour, **Hide settings** and **Delete workspace**. **Sharing** contains link and recipient controls. Its summary shows whether the link is on and how many people use it. **Unpublished changes** means your personal copy differs from the shared version; **Publish changes** updates that version.

**Edit on its desk** lets you arrange a workspace in place. **Save changes** keeps its current layout and notes. **Exit editing** keeps the previous saved layout and discards note edits. Preferences changes are saved immediately and are kept either way.

## Building one with MIO

Describe the desk and MIO builds it. For example: "A workspace for my editors with the posts list on the left half, Add New Post in the top right and Orders in the bottom right."

- MIO first reads every app this site has — admin menus, plugin apps, Trash and OpenStation's own apps — so it only uses what exists here.
- Asking for an app ("the Posts app on the left") opens the app itself, as its dock icon does, with all its tabs. Name a screen ("Add New Post") to open just that one.
- Windows are placed on a 6 × 6 grid, so a desk keeps its shape on any screen size.
- Only the apps you name — plus the ones its windows use — stay on the dock.
- It asks about the desk step by step — layout, dock apps, widgets, then notes — and you accept each one in the preview or in the chat.
- Notes are read-only messages on the desk for the people using it; they can dismiss them. An XL note is twice the size, for a longer welcome.
- The new workspace is a new desk; your main desk is not touched.

## Notes

On a workspace's card, **Edit on its desk**, then **+ Note** or **+ XL note** in the bar at the top. Type into the note, drag it by its pin, and **Save changes**.

## Positions

`full`, `left-half`, `right-half`, `top-half`, `bottom-half`, `top-left`, `top-right`, `bottom-left`, `bottom-right`, `left-third`, `center-third`, `right-third`, `left-two-thirds`, `right-two-thirds` — or exact cells: a column and row from 0 to 5, and how many columns and rows it spans.

## What MIO does not do

MIO does not delete workspaces, and does not create, change or turn off share links or release anyone using one. Those stay in the Workspaces window, where you can see who they affect.
