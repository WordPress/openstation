# Popup Siege side-console art brief 0.5.1

## Experience

- Game and mode: Popup Siege, queryless unscored arcade prototype.
- Desired emotion: a coveted 1998 web-surfing accessory attached to Mira's
  endangered homepage.
- Representative moment: the menu and active Breakout field with the console
  fixed at the right.
- Native review context: 960 × 800 standalone viewport; roughly 224 × 595
  sidebar in the historical OpenStation proof.
- Required empty zones: one live brand recess, one live 2 × 2 instrument
  recess, and four live control wells.

## Style contract

- Historical artifact: late-1990s translucent consumer-computer peripheral.
- Shape language: one narrow stepped appliance with two browser-side mounting
  lugs and three quiet functional zones.
- Palette roles: warm ivory ABS structure, translucent grape-purple perimeter,
  almost-black navy glass, and teal/grape/amber/red keys.
- Materials: fine injection-molded pebble texture, restrained translucent
  plastic thickness, dark screws, quiet glass.
- Lighting: restrained upper-left catalog light; no glow or cast shadow.
- Detail density: readable at game size; no ornamental seams or panel noise.
- Anti-era list: cyberpunk neon, modern esports hardware, rounded-card UI,
  spaceship panels, stone, marble, brushed metal, and blue glow.

## Accessibility and semantic invariants

- Every word, number, icon, label, pressed state, focus ring, and pointer target
  stays live DOM/CSS.
- The generated shell is decorative and suppressed in forced colors.
- The side relationship never becomes a stacked header.
- The geometry manifest owns normalized live regions and a truthful 500-pixel
  minimum appliance width for 44-pixel control targets.
