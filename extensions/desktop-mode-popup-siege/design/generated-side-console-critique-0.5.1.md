# Popup Siege generated side-console critique 0.5.1

Verdict: `pass` for prototype runtime after geometry correction.

## Five-pass review

- Composition: one compact identity window, one quiet instrument window, and
  one grouped four-key bank remain legible beside the playfield.
- Subject/identity: ivory ABS and a translucent grape rim evoke a 1998
  consumer-computer peripheral without turning into a modern gaming remote.
- Prop/world logic: the shell has exactly the expected brand recess, four-cell
  HUD, four keys, and two attachment lugs. It introduces no extra affordance.
- AI tells: no pseudo-text, fake logo, symbol, fused key, accidental face,
  unexplained badge, or high-frequency seam noise survives in the selected
  source.
- Production: the source and derivative are exactly 771 × 2041. The RGBA
  derivative has transparent corners, 5,672 partially transparent edge pixels,
  and no visible green-dominant pixels after soft-matte despill.

The first light study was superseded because it was 771 × 2039. The dark study
was rejected because its body lost contrast against the navy playfield and its
HUD/key geometry drifted too far for the shared live overlay.

The selected source also moved the painted key centers. The 0.5.1 geometry
manifest therefore uses a remeasured control region rather than inheriting
0.5.0 coordinates. Every word, value, icon, state, hit target, and focus ring
remains live DOM/CSS.
